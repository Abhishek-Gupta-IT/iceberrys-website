Welcome to Iceberry Valley

In a frozen world, one valley stays warm. A cave bear family and their neighbours grow up, go to school, work and solve everyday problems together, then paint each day's lesson on the Story Wall.

Coming 2027 on YouTube Ages 2 to 8 7-minute episodes
The world

One warm valley in an endless ice age

Hot springs keep Iceberry Valley green while glaciers and blizzards stay outside. Animals from across the frozen lands have settled here and built a village together. Inside the valley, the year has three seasons.

Frost

Snowy and cosy. Sledges, warm soup and long nights by the iceberry lamps.

Drip

Melting and muddy. Everything grows, and the beavers are busy at the dam.

Glow

The iceberries ripen and glow at night, and the aurora lights up the whole sky.

Everyone is a neighbour

Mammoths, rhinos, beavers, foxes, hares and owls live side by side. There are no villains, and nobody is ever in danger from anyone else.

Ice-age tools, no screens

Homes are carved from stone and timber. Clever inventions run on spring steam, pulleys and sledges, and the market works by trading.

Art is how the valley remembers

Every family paints on the Story Wall in the Great Cave, and the village puts on shadow plays at night.

What is an iceberry?

A glowing berry that grows only at the icy edge of the warm springs. It lights the lamps, dyes the paint and gives the valley its name.

Meet the family

The Iceberrys

The Iceberrys are cave bears, a real ice-age species with a high domed forehead and a short muzzle. Their fur is warm honey brown with frost-white tips on the ears, and the grown-ups grow tiny tufts of moss on their shoulders.

The village

Neighbours, school and work

Six more families live in the valley, each with a kid the same age as an Iceberry kid and a grown-up with an important job.

Woolly mammoths

The Oros

Tula is Juni's best friend: the biggest kid at school, gentle, and scared of breaking things. Her dad keeps the Warm Springs and her mum is the valley healer.

Woolly rhinos

The Rokas

They run the Bun Stall at the market. Roko is loud and loves to win.

Giant beavers

The Bebas

The valley's engineers build bridges, pulleys and the dam. Kiko is Tobo's best friend and a tireless inventor.

Arctic foxes

The Fens

New arrivals from the Far Ice. Papa Fen is the messenger, and Suki is still finding her feet.

Snow hares

The Hopsas

Mama Pia grows roots and grains on the terraces with her twelve kids. Nobody has ever counted them all correctly.

Ducks

Quack and Waddle

The twins fly in every Glow season, and everyone is sad when they leave.

Snowy owl

Teacher Ola

Runs the Little Den school. Patient, a little dramatic, and she loves a good question.

Musk ox

Grandpa Moku

Drives the school sledge. Grumpy on the outside and sweet underneath.

Every episode

It all ends at the Story Wall

Each 7-minute story teaches one small thing. At the end, the family paints the day's lesson on the cave wall in pictures, so any child in any language can follow along.

  1. 30 SECONDSA small problemSomething goes wrong, the way it does in real family life.
  2. 5 MINUTESThe adventureThe kids try to fix it themselves, with a little help from the grown-ups.
  3. 1 MINUTEThe turnSomeone notices a feeling, and everything changes.
  4. 30 SECONDSThe Story WallThe family paints what they learned, and the aurora seems to copy it.

For parents

Made to be gentle

The Iceberrys is made for children everywhere, with jokes and honest feelings for the grown-ups watching alongside them.

Pictures first

Every story makes sense with the sound off. There are no written words on screen, so it works in any language.

Soft on the eyes

Gentle pastel colours, slow camera moves and calm pacing, with no flashing or frantic cuts.

Real feelings

Waiting, losing, sharing, saying sorry and being brave. Grown-ups get tired and make mistakes too, and they say sorry.

For every family

No real-world countries, flags or religious holidays. The valley has its own festivals, like the Thaw Festival and the First Glow.
